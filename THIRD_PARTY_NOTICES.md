# Third-party material and rights boundaries

- `gordon-bell/` links to research papers, official conference records and other public sources. Bibliographic and performance records retain their provenance; links do not grant redistribution rights to papers.
- `notebooks/` contains only previously published, reviewed viewing assets. Notebook scans, thumbnails and PDFs are excluded from MIT. Existing redactions and the publication allowlist are retained. See `notebooks/LICENSING.md` and `notebooks/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- `hpci-policy/` links to MEXT, RIKEN, NDL and other public sources. Each source's conditions apply; this site does not grant permissions for linked documents, logos or institutional materials.
- `academic-roots/` retains its own notices and terms in `academic-roots/rights.html` and `academic-roots/LICENSE.txt`.
- Linked and quoted material retains the original rights and conditions. No third-party runtime or font is bundled by these three archives.

- `numerical-methods-atlas/` retains its MIT, content-scope and upstream KaTeX/Marked notices, including `vendor/licenses/`. Code licensing does not license cited papers or externally supplied content.
- `the-art-of-thermal-instability/` retains scientific provenance, model limitations and per-asset/source notices. Its copied public viewing/build outputs do not include private source PDFs.
- `guide/` contains bounded excerpts from the two public research sites, for source-grounded consultation; original texts and third-party sources retain their own conditions.

- `astronomy-step-by-step/` retains its MIT implementation-code license and separate content/third-party notices. Publisher and Open Library cover/bibliography links retain their providers' terms. Commercial textbook bodies are not bundled. Original educational models and figures retain their stated scope.
